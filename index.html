Ramesh Gupta, owner

Second generation, Sadar Bazar, Hapur

My father, Ganeshi Lal, started this shop in 1987 with one kadhai and a tin roof. I was twelve and my job was to stir the boondi when he went for namaz.

Today the shop is bigger, but the recipes are exactly his. We still start frying at 5 AM, and whatever is not sold by night goes to the temple langar, not to the next day’s tray.

When you come in, I would like you to taste first. Nobody should buy sweets they have not tried.
